Hexwipe Privacy Policy  
Effective Date: May 23, 2026  
At Hexwipe, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled within the Hexwipe iOS custom keyboard extension.
1. Personal Data Collection and Keystrokes  
Hexwipe does not collect, log, track, or monitor any personal data, text input, keystrokes, or personal information typed by the user. Everything you type remains completely private and local to your device.
2. Data Transmission and Internet Access  
Hexwipe does not require or utilize internet connectivity. No data generated, typed, or processed within the keyboard extension is ever transmitted over the internet or shared with third-party servers.
3. Purpose of Full Access (Open Access)  
Hexwipe requests Full Access solely to enable local integration features, such as pasting text from the system clipboard via the Paste key, and matching layout preferences across the application container. This permission is handled entirely locally on the user's device through standard iOS sandboxing APIs.
4. Children’s Privacy  
Because Hexwipe does not collect any personal information, it complies fully with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and does not gather data from children of any age.
5. Changes to This Policy  
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
